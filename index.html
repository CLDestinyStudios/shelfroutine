The math, step by step

Fictional example: 18 mugs on the shelf, 6 already promised.

  1. Available: 18 − 6 = 12
  2. Sales of 2 a day × 7-day lead time → reorder point 20, target 34
  3. Gap: 34 − 12 = 22
  4. Supplier sells cases of 12, minimum 24 → order 24
  5. Cost: 24 × $6.50 = $156

Reorder & Count Desk shows this math for every product, rounded to your supplier's case size. Then count stock without seeing the expected numbers first.
